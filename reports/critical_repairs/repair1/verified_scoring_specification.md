## 1. Master Composite Formula

The player reliability rating $R \in [0, 100]$ evaluates an individual cricketer's ability to consistently deliver positive leverage under high-pressure conditions across repeated matches.

$$R = 0.40 \cdot S_{\text{impact}} + 0.25 \cdot S_{\text{uplift}} + 0.20 \cdot S_{\text{consistency}} + 0.15 \cdot S_{\text{confidence}}$$

Where:
- $\sum w_j = 0.40 + 0.25 + 0.20 + 0.15 = 1.00$.
- Each individual sub-component $S_j \in [0, 100]$.
- $R$ is rounded to 2 decimal places: $\text{round}(R, 2)$.
- Candidates who do not meet sample-size eligibility requirements receive `NaN` for $R$.

## 2. Component Specifications

### 2.1 Component 1: Shrunk Pressure Impact ($S_{\text{impact}}$)

1. **Exact Source Function**: `compute_batter_reliability_scores` / `compute_bowler_reliability_scores` in `src/reliability/reliability_model.py`.
2. **Input Variable**: `shrunk_hp_combined_impact` (derived from `apply_empirical_bayes_shrinkage` in `src/reliability/uncertainty.py`).
   - For Batting: Net runs per ball above context expectation $\bar{y}_{\text{HP}}$, shrunk toward prior $\mu_0$.
   - For Bowling: Net runs prevented per ball above context expectation $\bar{y}_{\text{HP}}$, shrunk toward prior $\mu_0$.
3. **Mathematical Formula**:
   $$S_{\text{impact}} = \frac{100.0}{1.0 + \exp\left(-3.0 \cdot \hat{\theta}_{\text{shrunk}}\right)}$$
4. **Parameter Values**:
   - Logistic slope: $k_{\text{slope}} = 3.0$.
   - Empirical Bayes shrinkage noise: $k_0 = 20.0$ balls (batting), $k_0 = 24.0$ balls (bowling).
   - Shrinkage weight: $w_i = \frac{n_i}{n_i + k_0}$.
5. **Normalization & Centering**:
   - Centered at $\hat{\theta}_{\text{shrunk}} = 0.0 \implies S_{\text{impact}} = 50.0$.
   - $+0.50$ runs/ball $\implies S_{\text{impact}} \approx 81.76$.
   - $-0.50$ runs/ball $\implies S_{\text{impact}} \approx 18.24$.
6. **Output Scale**: Continuous range $(0.0, 100.0)$, rounded to 2 decimal places.
7. **Missing Data Behavior**: `fillna(0.0)`.

---

### 2.2 Component 2: Pressure Uplift ($S_{\text{uplift}}$)

1. **Exact Source Function**: `compute_batter_reliability_scores` / `compute_bowler_reliability_scores` in `src/reliability/reliability_model.py`.
2. **Input Variable**: 
   - Batting: `batting_pressure_uplift` = $\bar{y}_{\text{HP}} - \bar{y}_{\text{ord}}$ (runs/ball difference).
   - Bowling: `bowling_pressure_uplift` = $\bar{y}_{\text{HP}} - \bar{y}_{\text{ord}}$ (runs prevented/ball difference).
   - Generated by `compute_player_pressure_vs_ordinary` in `src/performance/player_baselines.py`.
3. **Mathematical Formula**:
   $$S_{\text{uplift}} = \frac{100.0}{1.0 + \exp\left(-3.5 \cdot \text{Uplift}\right)}$$
4. **Parameter Values**:
   - Logistic slope: $k_{\text{slope}} = 3.5$.
5. **Normalization & Centering**:
   - Centered at $\text{Uplift} = 0.0 \implies S_{\text{uplift}} = 50.0$.
   - $+0.30$ runs/ball elevation $\implies S_{\text{uplift}} \approx 74.08$.
   - $-0.30$ runs/ball regression $\implies S_{\text{uplift}} \approx 25.92$.
6. **Output Scale**: Continuous range $(0.0, 100.0)$, rounded to 2 decimal places.
7. **Missing Data Behavior**: `fillna(0.0)`.

---

### 2.3 Component 3: Match-Level Consistency ($S_{\text{consistency}}$)

1. **Exact Source Function**: `compute_batter_reliability_scores` / `compute_bowler_reliability_scores` in `src/reliability/reliability_model.py`.
2. **Input Variables**:
   - `positive_match_ratio` ($\text{PMR} \in [0.0, 1.0]$): Proportion of distinct high-pressure matches where player generated positive net impact ($> 0.0$).
   - `worst_quartile_match_impact` ($P_{25}$): 25th percentile of player's match-level impact distribution (worst-quartile floor).
   - Generated by `compute_match_level_consistency` in `src/reliability/consistency.py`.
3. **Mathematical Formula**:
   $$P_{25\text{ norm}} = \text{clip}\left(\frac{P_{25} + 1.0}{2.0}, 0.0, 1.0\right)$$
   $$S_{\text{consistency}} = 100.0 \times \left(0.60 \cdot \text{PMR} + 0.40 \cdot P_{25\text{ norm}}\right)$$
4. **Parameter Values**:
   - Weight on positive match ratio: $0.60$.
   - Weight on downside protection: $0.40$.
   - Linear scaling domain for $P_{25}$: $[-1.0, 1.0] \to [0.0, 1.0]$.
5. **Normalization & Centering**:
   - A player with 100% positive matches and $P_{25} \ge +1.0$ receives $100.0$.
   - A player with 50% positive matches and $P_{25} = 0.0$ receives $50.0$.
6. **Output Scale**: Bounded range $[0.0, 100.0]$, rounded to 2 decimal places.
7. **Missing Data Behavior**: `positive_match_ratio.fillna(0.0)`, `worst_quartile_match_impact.fillna(0.0)`.

---

### 2.4 Component 4: Statistical Confidence ($S_{\text{confidence}}$)

1. **Exact Source Function**: `compute_batter_reliability_scores` / `compute_bowler_reliability_scores` in `src/reliability/reliability_model.py`.
2. **Input Variables**:
   - `shrinkage_weight` ($w_i \in [0.0, 1.0]$): Sample depth weight from Empirical Bayes shrinkage.
   - `cluster_se` ($\text{SE}_i = s_i / \sqrt{m_i}$): Match-level cluster standard error from `compute_confidence_intervals` in `src/reliability/uncertainty.py`.
3. **Mathematical Formula**:
   $$S_{\text{confidence}} = 100.0 \times w_i \times \frac{1.0}{1.0 + \max\left(0.0, \text{SE}_i\right)}$$
4. **Parameter Values**:
   - Dampening denominator: $1.0 + \text{SE}_i$.
5. **Normalization & Centering**:
   - As sample size $n_i \to \infty$ ($w_i \to 1$) and between-match variance $s_i \to 0$ ($\text{SE} \to 0$), $S_{\text{confidence}} \to 100.0$.
   - For small sample ($n_i = 20 \implies w_i = 0.50$) with typical $\text{SE} = 0.33$, $S_{\text{confidence}} \approx 37.6$.
6. **Output Scale**: Bounded range $[0.0, 100.0]$, rounded to 2 decimal places.
7. **Missing Data Behavior**: `shrinkage_weight.fillna(0.0)`, `cluster_se.fillna(1.0)`.

## 3. Eligibility Qualification Rules

Defined in `src/reliability/eligibility.py` (`classify_batter_eligibility`, `classify_bowler_eligibility`):

| Discipline | Minimum HP Balls | Minimum HP Matches | Minimum Ordinary Balls | Total Evaluated | Total Qualified |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Batting** | $\ge 20$ balls faced | $\ge 3$ distinct matches | $\ge 25$ ordinary balls faced | 888 | 155 (17.5%) |
| **Bowling** | $\ge 24$ legal balls | $\ge 3$ distinct matches | $\ge 30$ ordinary legal balls | 647 | 270 (41.7%) |

## 4. Deterministic Tie-Breaking Specification

Defined in `src/ranking/tie_breaking.py` (`apply_deterministic_ranking`):

1. **Tier 1 (Primary)**: `reliability_score` descending.
2. **Tier 2 (Secondary)**: `shrunk_hp_combined_impact` descending.
3. **Tier 3 (Tertiary)**: `positive_match_ratio` descending.
4. **Tier 4 (Quaternary)**: `hp_matches` count descending.
5. **Tier 5 (Final Fallback)**: `player_id` (`striker_id` / `bowler_id`) ascending.
